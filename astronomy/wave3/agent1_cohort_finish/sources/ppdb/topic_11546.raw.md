ebellm | 2026-02-06 19:09:14 UTC | #2

Hi @nsabrams, thanks for your question.

1. Yes, all DIASources become alerts and appear in the PPDB.
2. There may be additional forced photometry measurements in the PPDB for a given DIAObject.  These are included in alert packets when available, but depending on the timing there may be forced measurements in the PPDB that have not yet appeared in an alert.
3. If the object is not changing, science - reference should yield zero flux in the difference image, and no DIASource will be created.
4. We don't yet believe that capacity constraints will prevent sending all alerts in crowded fields.
5. See ls.st/rtn-011 for discussion of the timeline of PPDB availability.
